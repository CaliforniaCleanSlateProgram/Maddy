## Funding Office proving ground

Funding is the immediate proving ground for Operational Consciousness.

Maddy must not behave like a grant search wrapper.

### Required funding cognition

Discover → read authoritative material → extract decision-grade evidence
→ verify → distinguish source from specific opportunity → understand
current cycle → reason against the Organization Package and Long-Term
Strategy → identify direct alignment → identify causal alignment →
identify legitimate adjacent alignment → determine eligibility now →
determine whether eligibility can be created → determine competitiveness
→ identify institutional gaps → work backward from future cycles →
compare against other opportunities → identify shared leverage → model
operational consequences → recommend → prepare execution → request
authority only where required → execute when authorized → verify → learn

### Funding lifecycle

Do not collapse all non-current opportunities into rejection.

At minimum distinguish:

-   **Active Pursuit** --- actionable now and qualified for executive
    attention.
-   **Positioning** --- not yet actionable/qualified, but early work can
    materially improve future eligibility or competitiveness.
-   **Watching** --- credible future trigger with insufficient current
    reason for substantial positioning work.
-   **Rejected** --- genuinely unsuitable, impossible, harmful, or
    unjustifiable strategic drift.

`executiveDeskReady: false` must never automatically mean discard.

### Adaptation standard

CCSP may legitimately evolve, add programs, create initiatives, build
capabilities, form partnerships, alter sequencing, or expand when the
change advances the mission and creates real value.

Do not encode a rule that CCSP may never adapt to funding.

Instead evaluate:

-   Is the connection real?
-   Is there a defensible causal chain?
-   Does the adaptation advance CCSP even if the grant is not won?
-   Is it achievable in time?
-   What else does it unlock?
-   What does it cost or strain?
-   Does it remain CCSP, or does it distort CCSP into an unrelated
    organization?

Example principle: upstream watershed work may create legitimate
alignment with downstream coastal/marine protection. Becoming an
unrelated elder-care institution merely to chase senior-services money
would be strategic drift.

## Executive Dispatch architecture

Permanent operating model:

Executive Director natural intent → Maddy / HUD → Executive Hallway →
appropriate offices, engines, providers, and work state → governed
execution → verified outcome/deliverable → Executive Hallway → Maddy /
HUD → Executive Director

The HUD is the primary executive dispatch center.

Specialized office screens exist for deep inspection and management, not
routine work.

The user should not become Maddy's file clerk, researcher, operator, or
intermediary when Maddy can perform the task through available tools and
authority.

## Human-task principle

Always ask:

> Can a capable human employee perform this action?

If yes, treat it as work Maddy should eventually be able to perform
herself through tools, workflows, integrations, providers, or embodied
systems.

This is a floor, not the Spooky ceiling.

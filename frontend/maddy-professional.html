A day with Maddy

She fits around the work you already have.

You do not need to become a prompt engineer or reorganize your company around another tool. Talk to Maddy naturally. Bring her into the moments where clarity, continuity, and follow-through matter.

Morning

Start already oriented.

Get caught up quickly instead of spending the first part of the day reconstructing yesterday.

You: “What did I leave unfinished?”
Maddy: “Two things are still moving. One can wait. One needs you this morning.”
Midday

Think before you react.

When something changes, use Maddy to sort the signal from the noise and decide what deserves your attention.

You: “This just changed. What does it affect?”
Maddy: “It changes the timing, not the goal. Here’s the cleanest next move.”
Later

Leave the thread intact.

Step away without mentally rehearsing everything you are afraid you will forget.

You: “Hold this for tomorrow.”
Maddy: “I’ve got the context. Tomorrow we can pick up from here.”

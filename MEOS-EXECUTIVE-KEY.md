# MEOS Executive Key

**System:** Maddy Executive Operating System (MEOS)  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Active Foundation Standard

---

# Purpose

The Executive Key is the official dictionary for the Maddy Executive Operating System.

Every Executive Office, mission, dashboard, report, workflow, and AI component
must use these standards.

---

# Universal Mission ID

Every mission receives a unique identifier.

Format:

PREFIX-000001

Examples:

G-000001
PD-000001
CSR-000001
OPS-000001

Mission numbers are never reused.

---

# Funding Prefixes

## G
Grant

Government, foundation, or nonprofit grant.

---

## PD
Private Donor

Individual donor.

---

## MG
Major Gift

Large individual contribution.

---

## CSR
Corporate Social Responsibility

Corporate giving programs.

Examples:

- Home Depot Foundation
- Lowe's Foundation
- Kaiser Permanente
- Google.org

---

## CP
Corporate Partnership

Long-term relationship with a company.

Examples:

- Equipment partner
- Annual sponsor
- Service provider
- Community partner

---

## CR
Corporate Request

One-time request made to a company.

Examples:

- Request trailer donation
- Request plumbing supplies
- Request nonprofit discount
- Request donated labor

---

## CF

Community Foundation

Regional or local foundations.

---

## SP

Sponsorship

Program or event sponsorship.

---

## GFM

GoFundMe Campaign

Crowdfunding.

---

## PP

PayPal Giving

PayPal fundraising.

---

## EV

Fundraising Event

Dinner
Auction
Golf Tournament
Community Event

---

## IN

In-Kind Donation

Goods
Services
Equipment
Professional Labor

# Mission Health

Measures how well the mission is being managed.

Includes:

Deadlines

Task Completion

Communication

Documentation

Office Workload

Compliance

---

# Mission Success

Measures the final outcome.

Examples:

Funding secured

Grant awarded

Donor obtained

Program launched

Partnership created

Mission completed

Mission Health and Mission Success are NOT the same thing.
